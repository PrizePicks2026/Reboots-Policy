Reboots

Protecting your picks from injuries.

You've got the perfect lineup locked in. Then a player goes down. Lineup over, right? Not so fast — for NBA, NFL, CFB, WNBA, and MLB, the Reboot policy can keep your square alive when an athlete leaves the game early.

01 · Applies to every eligible sport

The two rules that never change

Less → Win

WhyA Reboot only ever removes risk for a Less pick, never adds it — since the player left early, they can't add any more to the stat, so anyone who picked Less already has the best-case outcome locked in. Rebooting the square just confirms the win instead of leaving it exposed to a partial, uncertain stat line.

Graded normally

WhyOnce the athlete has already cleared the projection, the outcome of the square isn't uncertain anymore — the number was hit on the field, regardless of what happens after. There's nothing left for a Reboot to protect against, so the pick is graded the normal way: More wins, Less loses.

Members and agents can always refer to the official Reboots policy page for the current, up-to-date wording.
